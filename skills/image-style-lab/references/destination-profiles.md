# Destination profiles

Destination changes execution, not the evidence-backed identity of a Style or Tone.

## Portable

Keep platform assumptions minimal. Record what must stay recognisable and what the eventual destination still needs to decide.

## SEO banner

Define article relationship, headline policy, proof modality, wide and mobile crop behaviour, publisher mark, accessibility, and the page／blog handoff. Do not turn an article claim into a generated visual fact.

## Instagram carousel

Define cover-to-body rhythm, card roles, continuity, proof placement, CTA-card behaviour, mobile legibility, and carousel-safe assets. Card count and current copy belong to the Content Job, not the reusable Style.

## Instagram single image

Define one-message hierarchy, thumbnail recognition, subject／proof relationship, safe areas, caption boundary, and single-frame CTA behaviour.

## Multiple saved identities

One platform may contain multiple Styles and multiple Tones. Each has a unique ID, its own evidence, a clear intended use, and an independent approval status. Auto mode may recommend the nearest existing candidate but must not silently select, merge, overwrite, or register it.
