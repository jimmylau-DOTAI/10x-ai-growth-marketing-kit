# One-minute Reels beat guide

Use this only after the story route is chosen. It is a timing guide, not a fixed template.

| Beat | Typical share | Viewer job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hook | 0–6s | Recognise the tension or promised result immediately. |
| Proof／promise preview | 6–14s | Know what evidence, checklist or outcome will follow. |
| Explain | 14–40s | Learn two or three ordered ideas; each has a visible proof job. |
| Apply／self-check | 40–53s | Know what to check or do next. |
| Boundary＋CTA | 53–75s | Know the limit and one truthful next step. |

Keep spoken sentences short. Put only a 6–12-character conclusion on screen. A scene gets a new visual only when the idea or evidence changes.
